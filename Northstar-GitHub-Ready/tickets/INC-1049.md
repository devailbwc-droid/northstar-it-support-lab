INC-1049 — Finance shared drive access denied
Priority: P3 Normal | Status: Resolved
Requester: Ava Patel | Finance
Device: FIN-LT-009

Issue: After transferring from Sales to Finance, the requester could sign in but could not access the Finance share.

Investigation: Verified identity and reviewed account group membership. Identified that the account was missing the Finance-Read security group required for department access.

Root cause: Missing Finance-Read membership. After membership was added, the existing sign-in token still needed to be refreshed.

Resolution: Added the required Finance-Read membership and asked the requester to sign out and sign back in to refresh the security group token.

Verification: The requester opened the Finance share successfully and confirmed restored access.

Lesson learned: A group membership change may require a new sign-in before the user’s session reflects the updated permissions.
